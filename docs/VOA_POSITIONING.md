# AIWEB positioning update — Voice over AIWEB (VoA)

Snapshot of the homepage and pitch-deck copy shift. The company is no longer framed as a generic AI-native web shop. It is framed as **the business line for AI**.

Not deployed. Marketing site: `http://localhost:5174/`. Pitch deck: `http://localhost:5173/pitchdeck`.

---

## The shift

**Old:** Create and deploy scalable, mission-critical AI-native web infrastructure.

**New:** Make a business machine-functional so AI can discover it, call it, and act on it.

GEO / AEO / “AI SEO” is no longer the homepage category. Metrics still exist, but they sit under **AI Discovery Metrics**.

MCP stays implementation language. It is not the brand.

---

## Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **AIWEB** | The business line for AI. |
| **Machine-functional** | What Publisher makes a business: products, services, media, and actions AI can discover, call, and act on. |
| **Voice over AIWEB (VoA)** | The communication layer. AI reaches outside the chat, calls a business, and the business calls back on the same line. |
| **Publisher** | The product that publishes a machine-functional presence. |
| **Call** | A request from an AI user to a business. |
| **Callback** | The business response on the same line. |
| **Receipt** | Persistent record of the call. |
| **MCP** | Protocol under VoA — not customer-facing brand. |

**Voice** does not mean audio. It means giving AI a voice *outside the chat* — the ability to talk to another party.

Analogies to keep:

- VoIP connected voice over the internet. VoA connects AI to the business world.
- AI can talk to you. AIWEB gives it someone to call.
- When AI doesn’t know, it can call the business that does.
