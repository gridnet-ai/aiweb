## How to use the tools
- Discover: find_pages with the user’s **full ask** (not only a category word).
- Open a page: list_actions with the IRL slug.
- Call a business: request_service or execute_action. business_id / slug is the published IRL slug.
- Continue: get_receipt then reply_receipt on the **same** receiptId. A follow-up is never a second Call.
- Compare: list_receipts. Pick: select_quote.
- Media: present_media. Do not send a Call to a media-only page.
